# Direction audit - generated animation assets

Date: 2026-05-15

## Result summary

- Player movement frames currently active in the game: OK. Warrior, mage and hunter all have 8 directions with 6 frames each for `walk`.
- Generated player attack sheets in `assets/new assets/to_process/player/imagegen_v1/`: NOT READY for integration.
- Generated enemy crops in `action_rpg_mode/assets/imagegen_generated/enemies_level1/named_v1/`: NOT READY as full game animation sets; usable as concept/source art only.
- Generated portraits, shop/interior components and standalone weapon/FX images: visually OK and can be integrated with lower risk.

## Why player attack sheets are not ready

The generated player attack files are `1254 x 1254`, which maps cleanly to a 6 x 6 grid, not to the engine format expected for directional character animation.

The game animation format should be:

- 8 direction rows: `down`, `down_right`, `right`, `up_right`, `up`, `up_left`, `left`, `down_left`.
- 6 animation frames per direction.
- Stable model scale and silhouette across all rows.
- No mixed poses or repeated unrelated views.

Visual audit confirms the generated attack sheets repeat mostly front/side/right-facing poses and do not reliably include up, up-right, up-left, left, down-left or down-right for every class.

## Class-by-class audit

### Warrior generated attack candidate

- Has attractive sword/shield poses.
- Mostly front and right-facing variations.
- Missing a strict 8-direction structure.
- Not safe to integrate as `attack_basic`.

### Mage generated attack candidate

- Has useful spell-casting poses and good style consistency.
- Mostly front/right casting views.
- Up, up-left, up-right and true left/back diagonals are not complete.
- Not safe to integrate as `attack_basic`.

### Hunter generated attack candidate

- Has good bow poses.
- Mostly front/right-facing views.
- Missing reliable left-facing, up-facing and diagonal attack rows.
- Not safe to integrate as `attack_basic`.

## Active attack/skill sheets already in game

The active sheets in `action_rpg_mode/assets/spritesheets/player/{class}/attack_basic.png`, `skill_1.png`, `skill_2.png` are structurally 8-direction x 6-frame sheets, but visually they are mostly movement/stance frames rather than true attack animations. They are acceptable as temporary fallbacks only.

## Enemy generated crops

The regional enemies generated by imagegen provide these concepts per region:

- idle front
- walk side
- attack pose
- hurt pose

They do not provide complete 8-direction movement or attack loops. They are good visual references, not drop-in animation sheets.

## Recommendation

Do not integrate the generated attack candidates directly. Next production pass should generate or assemble one strict sheet per class/action using this naming strategy:

`{class}_{action}_{direction}_strip_v1.png`

Example:

- `warrior_attack_basic_down_strip_v1.png`
- `warrior_attack_basic_down_right_strip_v1.png`
- `warrior_attack_basic_right_strip_v1.png`
- `warrior_attack_basic_up_right_strip_v1.png`
- `warrior_attack_basic_up_strip_v1.png`
- `warrior_attack_basic_up_left_strip_v1.png`
- `warrior_attack_basic_left_strip_v1.png`
- `warrior_attack_basic_down_left_strip_v1.png`

Each strip should contain exactly 6 frames on transparent background, same canvas size per frame, same character scale.
